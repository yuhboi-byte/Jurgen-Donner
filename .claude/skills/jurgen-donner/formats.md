# Jurgen Donner: Formats and Pillars

## Account strategy

**Launch one account per platform (@jurgendonner), with three content pillars on it.** Do not launch three separate accounts on day one.

Why: each new account starts at zero, and three accounts means three times the production and posting work before you know what works. Early fans also get split three ways. The character is the niche. People follow Jurgen, not a format.

**Spin a pillar off into its own account** once it clearly wins: for example, it averages 3x the views of the other pillars over 20+ posts. At that point the main account promotes the spin-off and the new account starts with an audience.

**Do now:** reserve the handles on TikTok, Instagram, X, YouTube, and Facebook for the main name and spin-offs (`jurgendonner`, `jurgenreacts`, `donnerlab`, `donnerexplains`) so nobody squats them.

Pillar mix for the first 30 days: about 50% reactions, 25% knowledge, 25% memes. Adjust weekly based on views and watch time.

## Hook rules (all pillars)

The first 1.5 seconds decide everything, and most people watch muted.

1. **Visual weirdness before words.** Open on something strange already happening: an orange glove tapping a CRT, Klaus bumping his foot, Jurgen with the loupe down an inch from the lens.
2. **On-screen text hook** that works without sound: "German scientist sees a TikTok trend for the first time", "This man has been in his basement since 1998".
3. **Start mid-thought.** Never "Hello, today I will..." Start with "No. No no no. Klaus, come here."
4. **No intro, no logo, no greeting.**

## Pillar 1: Reactions (the growth engine)

**Format:** Jurgen in the lab watching a clip on the CRT. The clip plays on the TV screen or in a box in the upper part of the frame; Jurgen fills most of the frame and talks over it, pausing it to analyze.

**Structure:**
1. Hook: "Klaus. Come. Look at this." or an outraged reaction already in progress.
2. Clip plays, he pauses it at the key moment.
3. Scientific breakdown of what went wrong (physics, engineering, materials).
4. The human misreading: he gets the physics right and the people completely wrong.
5. Kelvin verdict.

**Best clip types:** fails, near misses, ambitious DIY, weird inventions, viral life hacks (he tests the logic), satisfying engineering, animals defeating humans, trends he does not understand.

**Copyright: read this before posting any reaction.**
- Reposting other people's clips is the biggest legal and reach risk in this whole project. Meta and TikTok both demote "unoriginal" reposts, and movie or TV clips get muted or struck by Content ID.
- Reactions are only defensible when the commentary is the main event. Jurgen must dominate the frame and talk for most of the runtime. Use only the few seconds of the clip you need.
- Avoid clips from movies, TV, music videos, and sports broadcasts. Use viral user clips, and credit the creator in the caption.
- On TikTok, the native **Stitch** feature is the cleanest legal route, but it cannot be automated through the API. Worth doing manually for the biggest clips.
- **Safest long-term option:** generate the "viral clip" itself with AI (a fictional fail video) and have Jurgen react to that. No copyright issue, fully automatable, and it adds to the "is this real?" effect.

### Example: reaction

```
PILLAR: reaction
HOOK: "German scientist reviews a trampoline backflip"
VISUAL OPENING: Extreme close-up, loupe down, orange finger pressed against the CRT glass.
SCRIPT:
  [not looking at camera]  Klaus. Come. Look at this.
  [Klaus bumps his foot]   Good boy.
  [clip plays: man attempts backflip off a trampoline into a pool, lands flat on his back on the grass]
  [pauses clip mid-air]    Stop. Here. Look at his knees.
                           He has the rotation of a refrigerator.
                           He needs 360 degrees. He has prepared for maybe 90.
  [resumes, impact]        Ja. Now we have the salad.
  [leans back, sincere]    And look. His friends are laughing.
                           In Germany we call this Fremdschämen.
                           I am embarrassed for him. Very much. In my body.
  [beat, flips loupe up]   But. He tried. This I respect.
                           Four hundred Kelvin. Warm. Not hot. Like soup.
ON-SCREEN CAPTIONS: "the rotation of a refrigerator" / "400 KELVIN"
POST CAPTION: He had the rotation of a refrigerator. 400 Kelvin. (clip: @creator) #science #fail #physics #germany
GENERATION NOTES: Lab set, CRT, Klaus yes. Clip on CRT screen.
```

## Pillar 2: Knowledge (the trust and follow engine)

**Format:** Jurgen at the chalkboard or workbench explaining a real fact. The fact is correct; his conclusion about humans is absurd.

**Rules:**
- Every fact must be true and verified before posting. One wrong fact in the comments kills credibility for the whole account.
- One fact per video. No lists of five.
- Mix science facts with German words and concepts (Fremdschämen, Kummerspeck). Those videos also work as "learn German" content, a big audience.

**Series names (consistency helps people follow):** "Things they do not teach you, number __", "German word for a feeling you have".

### Example: knowledge (science)

```
PILLAR: knowledge
HOOK: "Your banana is radioactive"
VISUAL OPENING: Jurgen holding a banana at arm's length with orange gloves, holding a Geiger counter beside it, deeply suspicious.
SCRIPT:
  [staring at banana]      This banana is radioactive.
                           Ja. Is true.
  [to camera]              Bananas have potassium. A small part of potassium is potassium-40.
                           Potassium-40 is radioactive. So when you eat a banana,
                           you receive a very small dose of radiation.
                           About 0.1 microsievert. Nothing. A flight across the ocean is much more.
  [beat]                   So the banana is safe.
  [slowly lowers banana]   But I have been eating one banana every morning for 40 years.
                           And I have never once been invited to a party.
                           I am not saying these things are connected.
  [puts banana in drawer, closes it]
                           This is science. You are welcome.
ON-SCREEN CAPTIONS: "potassium-40" / "0.1 microsievert" / "never invited to a party"
POST CAPTION: The banana is safe. I am not. #science #funfacts #bananas
GENERATION NOTES: Workbench, Geiger counter prop, Klaus no.
```

## Pillar 3: Memes (the shareability engine)

**Format:** short, visual, little or no talking. Built to be shared in group chats. The "is this real?" doubt is strongest here.

**Formats to test:**
- **NPC mode:** Jurgen running a "human behavior simulation," moving like a video game NPC, repeating one line, glitching when interrupted.
- **Dances:** he dances to a trend with total stiff precision, gloves on, completely serious, Klaus spinning beside him. Note: trending sounds are licensed only when added inside the TikTok/Instagram app. Posting via API means using original audio or a royalty-free track, or adding the sound by hand in-app.
- **Awkward interviews:** a fictional street reporter interviews Jurgen, or Jurgen interviews fictional passersby with a homemade microphone. All people on screen are generated fictional characters.
- **POV / skits:** "POV: you ask a German engineer to fix your Wi-Fi."
- **Inventions:** he demos a useless invention with total pride (the cold-on-both-sides pillow, prototype 214).

### Example: NPC mode

```
PILLAR: meme
HOOK: "German scientist tests NPC mode on himself"
VISUAL OPENING: Jurgen standing perfectly still in the lab, arms at his sides, eyes unfocused, fluorescent light flickering.
SCRIPT:
  [flat, looping]          Hello traveler. The basement is cold today.
  [Klaus bumps his foot; he resets with a jerk]
                           Hello traveler. The basement is cold today.
  [Klaus bumps him again; he glitches, head twitching]
                           Hello trav... Klaus. Klaus I am in the simulation.
  [Klaus leaves; long pause; back to flat voice]
                           Hello traveler. My son has left me.
ON-SCREEN CAPTIONS: "NPC MODE: ACTIVE" / "error: Klaus"
POST CAPTION: Simulation failed. Klaus has no respect for science. #npc #funny #robot
GENERATION NOTES: Wide shot, full body, Klaus yes. 15-20 seconds.
```

## Measuring what works

Track per post: views, average watch time, percentage watched to the end, shares, and follows from the post. Shares and completion rate matter more than likes. Every Sunday, keep the top 3 formats and drop the bottom 3.
